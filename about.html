ABOUT THE CLUB

City Club Mandya is a premier recreational and social club that brings together families, professionals, entrepreneurs and sports enthusiasts under one roof. Established with the vision of promoting sports, culture and community interaction, the club provides world-class facilities while preserving its traditional values and welcoming atmosphere.

The club offers excellent sporting facilities, elegant dining, comfortable accommodation, social events and family entertainment, making it one of the most prestigious clubs in the region. City Club Mandya continues to grow while maintaining its legacy of excellence, hospitality and service to its members.
